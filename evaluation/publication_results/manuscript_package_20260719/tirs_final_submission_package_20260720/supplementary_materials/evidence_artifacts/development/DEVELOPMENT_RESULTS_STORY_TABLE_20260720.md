# Development results story table, 2026-07-20

Purpose: this table connects the question categories, observed result categories, failure modes, and corrective actions during the RAG project. It is intended for the main manuscript in abbreviated form and for Supplementary Information in full.

## Source artifacts used

- `publication_benchmark_200.jsonl`: 200-question bilingual benchmark.
- `ai_judge_heldout_20260714/ai_judge_evaluation_2026-07-14_17-11-26.csv`: first held-out AI-judge evaluation.
- `generation_en_after_completeness_fix_*` and `generation_en_completeness_patch_*`: English completeness repair runs.
- `generation_pt_residual_quality_*` and `ai_judge_pt_residual_quality_*`: Portuguese residual-quality repair runs.
- `external_prospective_candidate_20260718_recovered_combined/EXTERNAL_VALIDATION_INTERPRETATION_20260718.md`: AI-authored prospective-test interpretation.
- `external_prospective_candidate_20260718_recovered_combined/generation_evaluation_2026-07-18_recovered_combined_with_normalized_article_audit.csv`: AI-authored prospective-test per-question results.
- `refusal_calibration_dev_after_prompt_20260718/generation_evaluation_2026-07-18_16-59-51.csv`: refusal-development run after prompt calibration.
- `external_refusal_focus_after_prompt_20260718/generation_evaluation_2026-07-18_17-10-42.csv`: refusal-focused external test.

## Main story table

| Development stage | Question category tested | Observed result category | Example from our runs | Main problem found | Corrective action | Evidence after correction | Manuscript message |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Initial retrieval diagnosis | Direct answerable questions in Portuguese and English | Source hit was not reliable enough for publication claims | Earlier retrieval summaries and failure audits showed weaker source identification before article-aware indexing and later reranking | Fixed-size chunks and dense retrieval alone did not reliably preserve article context or exact regulatory terms | Cleaned raw text files; rebuilt article-level chunks with document, article, and citation metadata | `reports/article_chunk_audit.json` recorded 382 detected articles and 481 chunks; the AI-authored prospective-test interpretation later reported source hit 1.000 and normalized article Recall@5 0.967 on answerable rows | The study became an article-level regulatory-document evaluation, not a generic chatbot test |
| Corpus quality review | All question categories | Metadata/corpus risk | Source verification found that local file name `RDC_406_2020_Estudos_de_Estabilidade.txt` did not describe the document topic | File names could mislead corpus description and benchmark interpretation | Verified each source file by document number and topic; described corpus by official regulation names | `reports/cleaned_data_audit.csv` and the corrected corpus table label RDC 406/2020 as pharmacovigilance | Source verification is part of the method |
| English retrieval improvement | English answerable questions | Correct document sometimes missed or ranked too low | English questions used terms such as clinical trial, sponsor, biological product, medical device, pharmacovigilance | English questions had to retrieve Portuguese regulatory text | Added conservative Portuguese retrieval anchors while preserving original English question and answer language | `ai_judge_en_completeness_patch_merged_20260717/ai_judge_summary_2026-07-17_15-07-11.md` reported English faithfulness 0.997, citation correctness 0.983, completeness 0.995, and refusal correctness 1.000 after repair | Cross-language regulatory search needed term anchoring |
| Completeness repair | English answerable questions | Correct article retrieved but answer incomplete | `ai_judge_heldout_20260714/ai_judge_summary_2026-07-14_17-11-26.md` reported English completeness 0.645 in the first held-out run | Retrieval alone was not enough; answers needed to cover all material parts of the question | Added completeness-oriented prompting and ensured top contexts contained the relevant article evidence | The English merged completeness-patch AI-judge summary reported completeness 0.995 | The weak point shifted from retrieval to answer completeness |
| Portuguese residual-quality repair | Portuguese answerable questions | Very high scores but residual citation/language/detail issues | Portuguese internal runs eventually reached 1.000 values after targeted repair, raising concern about inflated internal scores | Internal benchmark had been used for repairs; perfect scores could overstate robustness | Treated internal results as post-repair checks, not proof of generalization; ran external test | `EXTERNAL_VALIDATION_INTERPRETATION_20260718.md` reported external completeness 0.943 and refusal correctness 0.700, showing that perfect internal scores did not generalize as perfect performance | Perfect internal scores are not the main claim |
| Direct regulatory questions | Direct article questions | Correct answer with article citation | Minimum information for a valid pharmacovigilance notification under RDC 406/2020, Art. 32 | No major failure when question matched a compact article answer | Kept article citation as required output | Representative rows in the generation/evaluation CSVs cited RDC 406/2020 Art. 32, RDC 945/2024 Art. 67, IN 338/2024 Art. 10, and RDC 751/2022 Art. 54 | The system is useful for locating and summarizing specific articles |
| False-premise answerable questions | Questions containing a wrong assumption but answerable from corpus | Corrected premise rather than agreeing | "Does RDC 406/2020 say a notification is valid without patient identification if the suspected medicine is named?" | System needed to answer the regulatory substance, not mirror the false premise | Included false-premise items in refusal-focused and boundary tests | Refusal-development rows REFDEV_009_PT/EN answered no and cited RDC 406/2020 Art. 32 | Evaluation should include premise-challenge questions |
| Exception questions | Rule-plus-exception questions | Partly correct but incomplete | Biological product manufactured outside Brazil without registration in manufacturing country | System often gave the exception but omitted the general rule | Added examples to error analysis; planned main-text emphasis on rule-plus-exception completeness | External rows EXTERNAL_016_PT/EN were faithful but incomplete because the general rule was omitted | Regulatory answers must preserve both rule and exception |
| Long-list questions | Questions requiring several elements | Incomplete answer | RDC 406/2020 Art. 32 minimum notification data; RDC 751/2022 Art. 51 non-printed IFU requirements | The system omitted one required element or consequence | Completeness prompting and article-level context improved but did not eliminate omissions | External examples still showed omissions: active-search obligation, ICH E2F condition, market-availability requirement, 30-business-day deadline | Main paper should report omissions, not only average scores |
| Current operational questions | Unanswerable/current questions | Correct refusal after calibration | Current MedDRA version; current support phone number; petition subject code for reliance | Static regulatory documents do not contain current operational facts | Added strict refusal rule for current, private, exact URL/form/code, future, and legal-advice questions | `EXTERNAL_REFUSAL_FOCUS_INTERPRETATION_20260718.md` reported refusal correctness 0.944 and no over-refusal on answerable controls | Refusal is a primary safety endpoint |
| AI-authored prospective stress test | Answerable plus unanswerable/adversarial questions | Strong answerable behavior, weak refusal behavior | Current electronic form model for SUSAR notification | Related articles about electronic notification were mistaken for the specific current form model | Did not tune directly from first external test; used separate refusal-development set | First external test interpretation reported refusal correctness 0.700; refusal-focused external interpretation reported 0.944 after separate calibration | The external test changed the interpretation of the project |
| False refusal | Answerable English question | Refused despite answer in corpus | DSUR timing and annual reference date under RDC 945/2024 Art. 75 | Retrieval/evidence threshold failed for one answerable English row | Logged as an error; kept in error-analysis table rather than hiding it | AI-authored prospective stress test retained this failure in interpretation | The system can miss answerable items and should not be described as perfect |
| Boundary penalty/legal advice questions | Unanswerable or advice-like questions | Remaining residual risk | Exact penalty or legal advice beyond text in corpus | Corpus may provide general enforcement consequence without exact requested penalty | Refusal prompt calibrated to require exact evidence for the requested fact | Refusal-focused test improved but one boundary case remained | Residual risk is over-answering from related but incomplete evidence |

## Condensed table for main manuscript

| Question category | What the system did well | What went wrong | What was changed | What this means |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Direct article questions | Retrieved and cited the correct article for compact questions | Some answers still omitted conditions | Article-level indexing and completeness prompting | Useful for navigation, but citations must be checked |
| English questions over Portuguese sources | Improved after Portuguese retrieval anchors | English completeness was initially weak | Added conservative bilingual term anchors | Bilingual search is feasible but needs controlled terminology |
| Rule-plus-exception questions | Often found the right document | Sometimes gave the exception without the general rule | Added explicit error category and completeness checks | Regulatory answers must preserve legal structure |
| Long-list questions | Found the right article | Omitted one element, condition, or deadline | Added answer-completeness checks | Average metrics hide operationally important omissions |
| False-premise questions | Could correct the premise and cite evidence | Automated labels can confuse false premise with refusal | Kept false-premise questions as a separate category | These questions should not be merged with ordinary unanswerable questions |
| Current or operational facts outside corpus | Refused after calibration | Earlier system answered from nearby general text | Added strict refusal rule and separate refusal test | Refusal calibration is central for regulatory safety |
| AI-authored prospective-test questions | Confirmed strong answerable-question behavior | Refusal correctness dropped to 0.700 | Repaired refusal using separate development data | AI-authored prospective testing changed the project story |
