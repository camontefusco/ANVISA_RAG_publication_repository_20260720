Dear Editor,

We are pleased to submit the manuscript "Bilingual Retrieval-Augmented Access to ANVISA Regulatory Documents: A Technical Evaluation Using Drug-Development and Health-Product Questions" for consideration as a Research Article in Therapeutic Innovation & Regulatory Science.

This manuscript evaluates a bilingual retrieval-augmented generation prototype for article-level access to selected ANVISA regulatory documents. The study is framed as a technical regulatory-science evaluation, not as regulatory validation or a decision-support claim. The intended use is evidence navigation within a closed document set: helping users locate relevant document articles, summarize the answer, and refuse questions when the indexed corpus does not contain the requested information.

The work may be of interest to TIRS readers because it connects generative AI evaluation with practical regulatory activities in drug development, pharmacovigilance, biological products, medical devices, and reliance-based review. It also addresses a common cross-language situation: users may ask questions in English while the authoritative regulatory text is in Portuguese.

We include detailed supplementary methods, prompts, benchmark artifacts, per-question results, figure source tables, and checksums to support reproducibility and auditability. The code, benchmark manifests, generated outputs, prompts, and non-restricted derived artifacts will be made publicly available in a project repository after manuscript acceptance.

The authors declare no funding and no competing financial interests. Ethics approval was not required because the study used public regulatory documents and generated benchmark questions; no human participants, patient data, animal data, or clinical trial data were used.

Thank you for considering this manuscript.
