## Quality concerns to keep visible

- The study uses AI judging, not independent expert regulatory validation.
- Internal benchmark scores may be inflated because the system was improved after error review.
- Development-stage figures compare different evaluation sets and should not be described as paired statistical improvement.
- The intended use should remain evidence navigation, not autonomous regulatory decision-making.
- The corpus is selected and closed; it does not cover all ANVISA rules or current operational resources.

## Overall assessment

Scientifically and administratively, the package is close to submission-ready. The main remaining choice is whether the portal asks for DOCX only or DOCX plus PDF previews for text/tables/supplements. Figures should be uploaded as 600 dpi TIFF files unless the portal gives different instructions.
