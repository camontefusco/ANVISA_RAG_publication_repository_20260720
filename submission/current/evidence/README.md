# Current reproducibility evidence

Download revision_data.zip and open its 00_READ_ME_FIRST.md. STUDY_INDEX.csv gives the study-level map. MANUSCRIPT_DATA_MAP.csv links manuscript topics to datasets. FILE_INDEX.csv records all payload checksums.

This archive adds missing individual DR11 outputs, frozen experiment inputs, sibling-test review/evidence files and original Howard returns. Automated/provisional and human assessments remain separate. No scientific result or rating has changed. The separate Supplementary_Data_S16.zip is the dated-corpus subset and is also contained in revision_data.zip.

Historical scripts retain execution-time paths and are not automatically portable. Run VERIFY_PACKAGE.py after extraction to check hashes, DR11 counts/scoring arithmetic and sibling-review agreement without model calls. S16 has its separately documented portable count recalculation. Full raw corpora, indexes, credentials and concealed reviewer keys are excluded.
